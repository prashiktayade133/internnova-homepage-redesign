05 · FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long are InternNova internships?+

Programs are generally structured as 4–6 week learning experiences, depending on the track and cohort.

What domains are available?+

Current internship tracks include Web Development, Data Analytics, Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence, Python Programming and Java Development.

Will I receive a certificate?+

Eligible interns who complete the required assignments, projects, attendance and evaluation can receive an internship completion certificate.

Are the internships paid?+

Standard internships are unpaid unless a specific opportunity explicitly states otherwise.
